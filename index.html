DGMO: Training-Free Audio Source Separation through Diffusion-Guided Mask Optimization

Geonyoung Lee1, Geonhee Han1, Paul Hongsuck Seo1

1Korea University

Paper Code

Abstract

Language-Queried Audio Source Separation (LASS) enables open-vocabulary sound separation by conditioning on natural language descriptions. While existing LASS methods rely on task-specific training, we explore whether pretrained diffusion models can inherently perform separation without additional training. We introduce a training-free framework that leverages generative priors for zero-shot LASS, identifying key limitations and proposing Diffusion-Guided Mask Optimization (DGMO). This test-time optimization framework refines spectrogram masks for precise, input-aligned separation—repurposing diffusion models for zero-shot separation.

Diffusion-Guided Mask Optimization

Our novel training-free LASS framework overcomes limitations of prior methods by integrating mask optimization and generative refinement. It operates in two stages: reference generation and mask optimization.
